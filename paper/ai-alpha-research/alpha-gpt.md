pp. 196-206. [Publisher record](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-demos.14/)

## Reading observation

The system connects human investment ideas to LLM-assisted alpha construction
and iterative feedback. The paper evaluates several stages rather than only
presenting a chat interface. Its competition evidence is author-reported;
section 5.4 / Table 2 is a dated IQC 2024 Stage 2 snapshot, not a personal
account or final China ranking verified by this note.

## Our assessment and next question

We find the idea-to-system narrative inspirational. Our own research should
make every transformation equally inspectable: what was intended, what was
implemented, what the test rejected, and what remains uncertain.

We would not accept readable code or a favorable model judge as a semantic
oracle. Before using an analogous workflow in PWOL, require independently
calculated toy examples, timestamp checks and a sealed evaluation set. Compare
feedback with a fixed-prompt baseline at matched computational budgets; record
human review time separately. This is our proposed test, **NOT RUN**.

Publication verifies the scholarly record, not independent tradability of our
alphas. [Public reflection](../../report/2026-11-15-alpha-gpt-pengyi166/README.md)
and [rights/provenance](PROVENANCE.md).
