# From an alpha archive to falsifiable AI research

## What Alpha-GPT inspires us to build around Pengyi166

**Author:** Pengyi Peng (PNGY), with Codex editorial assistance.
**Date / last updated:** 2026-11-15 (Asia/Shanghai).
**Status:** selected public methodological essay; not peer reviewed.
**Origin:** proprietary **P University / P Lab**, PAPER-PWOL-001 (internal

## 1. An inspiration, not a claim of equivalence

We find Hang Yuan and his coauthors' work inspirational because it treats an
investment idea as something that must survive an explicit research process.
Our reading set is [Alpha-GPT](../../paper/ai-alpha-research/alpha-gpt.md),
[Alpha-GPT 2.0](../../paper/ai-alpha-research/alpha-gpt-2.md) and
[QuantAgent](../../paper/ai-alpha-research/quantagent.md).
Their publication states are different; see the source cards rather than
assuming all three were accepted by EMNLP.

Our editorial assessment is that a strong paper gives the reader a problem
worth caring about, a mechanism worth inspecting, evidence worth questioning
and limitations worth acting on. We want to learn that style of argument.
It is more useful than attaching an agent label to every existing script.

The lesson we take is a writing discipline: state the question before the
architecture; state the contract before the output; state the counterexample
before the claim of improvement. An attractive diagram is not an experiment.
A fluent explanation is not an independent check of an economic mechanism.

## 2. Where PWOL and Pengyi166 enter

**PWOL** is our proprietary alpha-research home. **Pengyi166** is the historical
index of **166 WorldQuant BRAIN submissions** associated with our **2024 IQC /
MAPC** research period. This author-owned historical description is not a new
per-record competition audit conducted for this essay.

The archive is useful as a collection of research questions, implementation
decisions and failures to revisit. It is not evidence of 166 independent,
profitable, reproducible or simultaneously tradable strategies. Correlated
variants are not independent hypotheses. Counting submissions cannot establish
an advantage over another team's paper or competition system.

The source audit retained adverse proxy outcomes; the small hindsight-selected
stock proxy was not a reproduction of the original platform strategies. We
retain that negative finding here rather than exporting private return tables
or using the earlier favorable period as a performance advertisement.
Independent original-166 economic replication remains **NOT ESTABLISHED**.

We have research scripts and synthetic evaluation artifacts, but this essay
does not establish a continuously autonomous, profitable end-to-end alpha
pipeline or a causal AI productivity gain. Those are different experiments.

## 3. What we selected from the internal work

The following is a rewritten methods selection, not the private draft itself.
P University / P Lab is an independent personal research initiative, not an
accredited or degree-granting university. Cited authors are not coauthors of
this essay, collaborators or endorsers.

| Evidence class | What it can support | What it cannot support alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Historical archive | What was recorded or submitted | Independent hypothesis count or future profit |
| Platform-reported result | A platform observation under its settings | Our reconstruction of raw data and exact semantics |
| Derived proxy | An explicitly different, bounded experiment | Equivalence to the original strategy |
| Synthetic control | Known-answer semantics and software invariants | Real-market predictive validity |
| Independent PIT experiment | A dated, licensed reconstruction within scope | Unlimited future validity or production approval |

These classes must not be pooled into a single impressive-looking score. A
research release needs separate verdicts for implementation, empirical
validity and permission to deploy. A software pass is not an economic pass.

## 4. Our proposed contract for an AI alpha workflow

This is our proposed design, not an implemented reproduction of these papers:

```text
Idea -> hypothesis card -> semantic/data contract -> independent tiny oracle
     -> point-in-time features -> frozen experiment -> costs and uncertainty
     -> falsification/negative controls -> human review -> scoped claim
```

A hypothesis card should name the economic intuition, plausible opposite sign,
universe, horizon, earliest tradable time and rejection criteria. The semantic
contract should specify units, windows, adjustment conventions, membership and
when information was actually available. Signal generation must not read its
future label. A late correction is a new version, not silently available history.

Every transition should preserve source, input fingerprint, code revision,
configuration and failure reason. A missing licensed field must produce a
blocked experiment, not a similar-sounding substitute labeled "reproduction."
The portfolio layer needs exposures, turnover, costs and trading constraints;
single-signal statistics cannot silently stand in for portfolio returns.

The most valuable feedback may be a rejection. Store why an idea failed and
which assumption the evidence tested. Do not let an agent turn a failure into
a success by changing the universe, sign, horizon and test period afterward.

## 5. A bounded experiment we could run next

**Question:** Does feedback improve semantic correctness and fault detection
over a fixed prompt, at a fixed computational budget?

Start with three author-created synthetic tasks: a lagged feature with known
answers, an as-of revision join, and a cross-sectional membership change.
These are a protocol pilot, not a statistically convincing trading benchmark.
No protected expression or original platform field is required.

Freeze prompts, fixtures, oracle answers, development split, final audit split,
seed, model/version and resource caps before execution. Compare three arms:
fixed prompt; iterative internal critique; critique plus development-only
external test feedback. Use the same tasks and caps per arm. Human reference
answers are semantic oracles; log human minutes separately, not as equivalent
tokens. A larger predeclared task set is needed for uncertainty estimates.

Report correctness against independently calculated answers, detection of
deliberate defects, false rejection rate, reproducibility, latency, tokens,
cost and review time separately. Do not hide a failed safety check inside a
weighted aggregate. Freeze memory before the audit; injecting audit labels
into memory must be detected as contamination, not counted as learning.

Acceptance would require a prespecified improvement with no leakage regression
and a defensible cost trade-off. Repeatedly opening the final audit invalidates
its held-out status. Report every attempted configuration, including null or
negative outcomes. Any real-market extension requires licensed PIT data,
survivorship-aware membership, costs and a new untouched evaluation period.

**Pilot status: NOT RUN. AI improvement: UNMEASURED. Economic validity: NOT
ESTABLISHED. Production/live trading: NO-GO.** This release tests documents,
metadata and publication boundaries, not an alpha-generation model.

## 6. The standard we want our next paper to meet

Our aspiration is not "166 alphas plus AI equals a validated system." It is a
smaller, defensible contribution: one precise question, one runnable artifact,
one honest comparison and one retained counterexample. Public methods can
invite useful criticism while the protected archive remains proprietary.

We welcome discussion about semantic oracles, fair resource budgets, adaptive
overfitting and how to distinguish helpful feedback from holdout leakage.
That is a concrete way to learn from excellent research communication without
claiming to have replicated its results or inherited its authors' endorsement.
