## Title

📢 MAJOR direction: Evaluation (Benchmark) as our core research focus — and introducing PAT (Pengyi Agent Team)

## Body

**P-Research is taking a major directional step: benchmark / evaluation is now our primary deep-research focus.**

Evaluation is the feedback channel of self-evolution — without eval, adaptation is a random walk. From now on, every research direction starts with one question: *how do we measure it?* Reproducible benchmarks, not leaderboards.

**Introducing PAT (Pengyi Agent Team)** — our internal Agent Team operating five capability modules:

```
RAG · Memory · Tool Use · Planning · Evaluation
```

Five Foundation Agents, human-approved, under one philosophy: **open frameworks, closed details**.

**The relationship:** P-Research is to PAT what dsh-quant is to our quant research teams (PDAT–PAAT–PCPT–PRT–PET) — the open-source arm where PAT's open research lives.

**Agentic-first.** We are deeply interested in agentic research — multi-agent, swarm intelligence, agent evaluation — and P-Research will prioritize agentic studies in close collaboration with PAT.
