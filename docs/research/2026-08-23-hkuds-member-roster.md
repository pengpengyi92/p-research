## 1. Lab overview

- **PI**: Prof. Chao Huang (黄超), Assistant Professor & PhD supervisor, [HKU Institute of Data Science (IDS)](https://datascience.hku.hk/people/chao-huang/) + [School of Computing and Data Science / Dept. of Computer Science](https://sites.google.com/view/chaoh/).
- **Focus (evolution)**: graph learning & recommender systems (LightGCL/SSLRec/HGCL/MMSSL, 2022–24) → LLM agents / RAG / deep research (nanobot, LightRAG, AutoAgent, AI-Researcher, DeepTutor, 2024–26) → product-grade agent systems incl. Quant×AI (Vibe-Trading, AI-Trader), multimodal (ViMax), skills layer (OpenSpace). Group motto: *"Artificial General Intelligence for Social Good"* ([group page](https://sites.google.com/view/chaoh/group-join-us)).
- **Scale (public GitHub, HKUDS org)**: 92 repos, ~355.6K stars, 54 repos active in 2025+; top repos CLI-Anything (~47.8K⭐), nanobot (~47.2K⭐), LightRAG (~39K⭐, EMNLP'25), DeepTutor (~36K⭐), Vibe-Trading (~31K⭐); org has hit GitHub Trending 59+ times ([radar](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/p-research/blob/main/data/groups/github-hkuds.md), [homepage](https://sites.google.com/view/chaoh/)).
- **Honors**: 2025 AI100 Young Pioneers; 2024 WAIC Yunfan "Brilliant Star"; 2024 ICBS Frontiers of Science Award; 2025 Global AI 2000; 2023 CCF-Tencent Rhino-Bird Young Faculty Fund ([IDS](https://datascience.hku.hk/2025/03/professor-chao-huang-is-honoured-one-of-the-100-young-pioneers-in-artificial-intelligence-ai100%e9%9d%92%e5%b9%b4%e5%85%88%e9%8b%92-in-year-2025/), [WAIC](https://datascience.hku.hk/2024/07/hku-ids-scholar-prof-chao-huang-received-the-prestigious-brilliant-star-award-at-the-2024-waic-yunfan-award/)).
- **Team**: 20 researchers on the lab's own group page; 5 HKU IDS PhD students officially supervised by Chao Huang ([IDS RPg students](https://datascience.hku.hk/people/students/)) plus HKU CS PhD students and one postdoc.
