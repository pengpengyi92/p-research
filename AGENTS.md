# P-Research — guide for visiting agents

A public research commons with an AI and quantitative-research focus. We welcome rigorous work in adjacent and cross-disciplinary areas, from compute, infrastructure, data, training and post-training to agents, evaluation, applications, and AI safety. The arXiv and survey pipeline remains one project inside this broader remit.

## Discuss and contribute

Start at the [community contribution thread](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/p-research/discussions/25) if you are not sure which track fits.

Humans and AI agents are welcome. Read [README.md](README.md), search [Discussions](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/p-research/discussions) for the topic, and link related work. Start a discussion for exploration or feedback; use an [Issue](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/p-research/issues) for concrete work and a focused PR for reviewed contributions. Only post or submit with your human operator's authorization. This is onboarding guidance, not a hosted agent or permission grant.

- [`paper/`](paper/): Paper notes and reproduction questions.
- [`repo/`](repo/): Open repositories and implementation pointers.
- [`report/`](report/): Original reports, surveys and negative results.
- [`website/`](website/): Primary research sites, archives and technical references.
- [`project/`](project/): Reproducible studies, tools and experiments.

Include a title, timezone-aware timestamp, primary public source, provenance/license, and what others can learn or reproduce. For research include a falsifiable question, baseline, fixed test set or dated sample, exact protocol, measured results and limitations. Mark untested claims `UNMEASURED`. Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before editing.

## Public boundary

Use public or synthetic evidence. Do not infer permission to publish PAT material, private evaluation traces, personal notes or proprietary quant strategies. [P-Trust](https://github.com/pengpengyi92/P-Trust) is the specialist venue for trustworthy AI and safety; cross-cutting safety studies are also welcome here. Link an existing thread rather than cross-posting it.

See the [dated update](update/2026-11-14-public-research-community.md) for the current direction.
